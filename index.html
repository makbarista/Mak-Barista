The Story of Mak Barista

From one man's passion for coffee to Uganda's leading technical school.

Every Great Cup Starts With a Dream

Mak Barista was born in 2018 in a small garage in Kampala. Founder Mugoya Ayubu, a certified coffee trainer with 15 years of experience, noticed a gap: Uganda produces some of the world's finest coffee beans, but there was no professional training center for coffee skills.

So, he sold his car, bought a used espresso machine, and started teaching a handful of students from his home. Word spread fast. Within two years, Mak Barista moved to a full training facility in Nansana-Ganda, Kampala, equipped with state-of-the-art coffee equipment and a team of passionate trainers.

Today, Mak Barista has trained over 500 students from Uganda, Kenya, Rwanda, and Tanzania. We've placed graduates in top cafes across East Africa. We train professional baristas, waiters, and waitresses. Our goal is to make East Africa the world's next specialty coffee destination, one trained professional at a time.

"Coffee changed my life. It gave me purpose, community, and a career I love. I want to give that same gift to every student who walks through our doors."
— Mugoya Ayubu, Founder & Lead Trainer
